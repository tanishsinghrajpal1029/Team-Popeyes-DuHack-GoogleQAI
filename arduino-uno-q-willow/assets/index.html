WHY IT MATTERS
—

A few extra SWAPs can beat one very unreliable coupler.

Teaching model: each move before the final interaction approximates one SWAP = three CZ gates. Estimated survival multiplies (1 − error) across those gates. It omits decoherence, crosstalk, readout error, parallel schedules, and calibrated compilation.
